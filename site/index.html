New in 1.3

Invisible when you share your screen.

Turn on one switch and Sill Note disappears from screen recordings, screen sharing and screenshots — Zoom, Teams, Discord, OBS. You still see it as usual, so your notes stay with you even in the middle of a call.

Settings → Privacy → Hide from screen sharing. Works on Windows 10 (version 2004) and later. It can’t hide your screen from a camera or a video capture card.

New · Opacity

See through it.

Make the panel’s background see-through, anywhere from 60% to 100%. Text, images and buttons stay crisp — you just see a little of what’s underneath. Settings → Appearance → Opacity.

New · Focus

Hide the list. Give your note room.

Click the sidebar button in the top-left corner and the note list slides away, so your note fills the whole panel. Click again to bring it back.

Media

Images, audio and YouTube. In one note.

Drag in an image, drop an audio file or paste a YouTube link. It all sits right inside your note, next to your text.

Voice notes

Just talk. It’s saved.

One click starts a voice note with a live waveform. Switch microphones mid-recording. The mic turns off the moment you stop.

Organize

Two levels of tabs. In color.

Topics across the top, notes on the left. Color, rename and reorder them. Light and dark themes included.

Privacy

Your notes stay yours.

No account, no cloud, no tracking. Everything is stored on your computer. Read the privacy policy.

Good to know

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

The direct download isn’t code-signed yet, so Windows warns the first time. Click More info → Run anyway. The upcoming Microsoft Store version is signed by Microsoft and won’t show this.

How do I open it?

Rest your mouse on the middle of the right screen edge, or press Ctrl + Alt + N. You can change the shortcut in Settings.

Does it connect to the internet?

Only when you paste a YouTube link: Sill Note fetches that video’s thumbnail and title once. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy.

Which file should I download?

Download for Windows is right for most people: it’s the installer, needs no administrator rights and adds Sill Note to the Start menu. Installer (ZIP) is the same file, compressed — handy if your browser or email service blocks .exe downloads. The portable version needs no installation: unzip it into any folder and double-click Sill.exe. Your notes are stored in the same place with all three.

Can I see what Sill Note actually does?

Yes. The complete source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can read exactly what the app does. There are no hidden connections, no tracking and no background uploads — the only time it goes online is the optional YouTube preview. The Microsoft Store version is also reviewed by Microsoft before it’s published.
